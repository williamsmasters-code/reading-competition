About the competition

The Michelle Coy Reading Competition celebrates the young readers of Carriacou and Petite Martinique, and honours a teacher who believed in every one of them.

Michelle Coy, known to her students as “Teacher Michelle”, was the Reading Specialist for Carriacou and Petite Martinique. Her teaching career spanned more than a decade and took her to all six primary schools on the sister islands. She played a key role in the Caribbean Centre of Excellence for Teacher Training programme, and she worked to make sure our children were included in the regional Courts OECS Reading Competition.

Teacher Michelle passed away in November 2009. To honour her work, the Mt. Pleasant and Grand-Bay Organization USA, a New York based group, sponsored a reading competition in her name, held together with the Division of Education.

Today the competition keeps her dream alive: confident, joyful readers in every school on our islands.

2025 Winner 🏆

On 4 November 2025 in Hillsborough, Carriacou, Azariah Williams of Our Lady of the Rosary Roman Catholic School was crowned champion of the 11th Annual Michelle Coy Reading Competition.

The event honours the late reading specialist Michelle Coy. It was organized by the Mt. Pleasant and Grand Bay USA Organization. Cynthia McIntosh-Noel, President of the organization, noted that they have been serving the tri-island state for over 30 years.

Permanent Secretary Leanwall Perrotte spoke about the importance of reading, and about education during the Revolution. Area Education Officer Delon Moses said the competition highlights how books can unlock creativity and growth.
