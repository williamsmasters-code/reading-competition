MICHELLE COY READING COMPETITION - LOGIN EMAIL TEMPLATE (paste into Supabase)

Only ONE template is needed: "Reset password". The Site Admin console uses it both when
it makes a new account and when it resets a password. The wording changes by itself
("Welcome! ..." for a new account, "... set a new password ..." for a reset).
The Supabase connector cannot change email templates, so these steps are done by hand.

WHAT THE EMAIL SAYS
  - the website link (https://williamsmasters-code.github.io/reading-competition/)
  - their login email
  - their role (Judge, Head Judge or Site Admin)
  - an optional "Choose your own password" button
  It never contains the password. The password the Site Admin types works at once and
  keeps working until the person changes it; the button does not cancel it.

STEPS
  1. Open https://supabase.com/dashboard and pick the project MichelleCoyReading.
  2. Left menu: Authentication > Emails > Templates (on some screens: "Email Templates").
  3. Click "Reset password" (sometimes called "Reset Password" or "Recovery").
  4. Subject heading: paste exactly
         Your login for the Michelle Coy Reading Competition
  5. Message body: switch to the source/HTML view if there is one, select everything
     that is there now and delete it, then paste the whole of reset-password.html
     (open it in Notepad, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V in Supabase).
  6. Click Save.
  7. Leave the other templates ("Invite user", "Confirm signup", "Magic link",
     "Change email address", "Reauthentication") as they are. They are not used for
     account creation or password resets.
  8. Check Authentication > URL Configuration: Redirect URLs must include
         https://williamsmasters-code.github.io/reading-competition/**
     (already set up earlier). Without it the button would open the wrong page.

LIMITS OF SUPABASE'S BUILT-IN EMAIL (no custom SMTP)
  - About 2 emails an hour for the whole project. If the limit is reached, the account
    or new password is still saved; the console says the email was not sent. Tell the
    person their email and password yourself, or untick "Send login email".
  - IMPORTANT: Supabase's built-in email only delivers to people who are members of the
    Supabase organization's Team (Organization settings > Team). Emails to anyone else
    are refused ("Email address not authorized"). To email the judges, either invite
    each judge to the Supabase team (this gives them access to the Supabase dashboard,
    so it is not recommended), or set up a custom email sender (SMTP) under
    Authentication > Emails > SMTP Settings. Until then, tell judges their login
    yourself. The console shows a clear message when Supabase refuses an address.
  - The "Choose your own password" button works once and expires after 1 hour.
